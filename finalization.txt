What if a robot "demo" wasn't just a video to watch, but a real demonstration you could interact with and probe?

We are excited to introduce RoboMesh, a web-based platform that connects people to real robots through online interaction.

- Real-robot interaction: remotely interact with robots through natural interfaces (language commands, voice, clicking, dragging, pointing).
- From watching to testing: instead of only seeing curated success cases, actively try the system and observe how robots respond to diverse inputs in real time.
- Beyond demos, toward diagnosis: logging, replay, and analysis of real robot interactions, including both successful trials and failures.

We are calling for users:
- For developers: answer key failure-analysis and HRI questions.
- For reviewers: failed executions can be replayed, inspected, and exported, for transparency.
- For labs: user commands, robot observations, system responses, and recovery behaviors can be collected as valuable data.

Try RoboMesh: https://robomesh.ssilabs.org
